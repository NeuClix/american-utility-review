How it works

Four steps from link to reward

  1. Sign up

    It takes a minute. We email you a private dashboard with your personal link.

  2. Share

    Send your link by text, email or social. Your dashboard has ready-made messages and a QR code.

  3. We review their bills

    Anyone who requests a review through your link is credited to you for 90 days.

  4. Get paid

    When your referral becomes a client and pays AUR, your reward is released. Track every step on your dashboard.
