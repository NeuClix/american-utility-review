# American Utility Review – Client Guide

This guide is for businesses and organizations requesting a utility bill review from American Utility Review (AUR). It explains what AUR does, each step of getting started, what documents to provide, how your information is protected, and answers to common questions.

---

## 1. What AUR does

American Utility Review is a team of independent commercial utility auditors, reviewing utility bills since 1986 (originally as Utility Review Consultants LLC).

We review your **electric, natural gas and water/sewer bills** to:
- find **billing errors and overcharges**, and refunds you may be owed;
- confirm you are on the **proper rate** under the utility's tariffs and riders;
- check **taxes, fees, meter readings and demand charges**;
- recommend ways to **save going forward**.

Our review focuses strictly on billing, not on operational changes.

**Who we work with:** manufacturers, restaurants, hotels, hospitals, schools and universities, churches, local, county and state governments, agriculture, oil and gas producers, pipeline companies, retail and other businesses of all sizes, nationwide.

## 2. Getting started, step by step

### Step 1: Request a free review
Go to **aur.neuclix.com**, click **Request a free bill review**, and enter your name, organization, and an email or phone number. It takes under a minute.

### Step 2: Upload your bills
Right after submitting, you're taken to your **private upload page**.
- Drag files into the box, or tap **choose files**.
- The **last 12 months** of electric, gas and water/sewer bills is ideal, but a recent month of bills is enough to start.
- PDFs, photos and scans all work, as do Excel, CSV and Word files, up to **10 MB per file**.
- On a phone, you can take photos of paper bills.
- Each file shows **Uploaded ✓** when it's received.
- You can come back to the same page later to add more files, using the link from your confirmation.

### Step 3: Sign the Letter of Authorization
On the upload page, click **Sign the authorization form**. Your name and company are filled in for you. The form asks for:
- your state, county and city;
- your name and title, and your company's legal name;
- your **utility account number(s)** and **service address**;
- the date, and your typed name as your digital signature.

The Letter of Authorization lets AUR request your billing history and rate information directly from your utility companies. It **does not change your service or your account**, and it doesn't let AUR sign up for or cancel services. You can revoke it at any time with written notice.

### Step 4: We review your bills
Our auditors review your bills and billing history line by line: rates and tariffs, demand charges, meter readings and multipliers, power factor, fuel adjustments, taxes, franchise fees and surcharges.

### Step 5: We share what we find
We walk you through any refunds and savings opportunities we identify.

### Step 6: You decide
Nothing changes without your approval. If you choose to move forward, we pursue refunds and rate changes on your behalf.

## 3. How AUR is paid

- There is **no upfront cost** to request a review.
- AUR works on a **shared-savings basis**: we are paid a share of refunds and savings only after you receive them. If we don't find refunds or savings, you owe nothing.
- The exact terms are set out in your agreement before any work that involves a fee.

---

## 4. Your documents and privacy

- Uploaded files go directly to AUR's private, secure storage. Only AUR's auditors can see them.
- We use your bills only to review your utility costs, and never share or sell them.
- Keep your upload page link private: anyone with it can add files to your review.
- **AUR will never ask for payment information to release a refund.** Refunds come from your utility company or the government agency involved. If anyone claiming to be AUR or your utility asks for payment to release a refund, don't pay; contact us.

## 5. Frequently asked questions

**How long does a review take?** It depends on how many accounts you have and how quickly utilities provide billing history. We'll keep you updated.

**Which bills should I send?** Electric, natural gas and water/sewer. The last 12 months is ideal; one recent month of each is enough to start.

**My file won't upload.** Check that it's a PDF, image, Excel, CSV or Word file under 10 MB. Very large scans can be split or emailed. If the page says the link isn't valid, contact us for a new one.

**I have several locations or accounts.** List every account number and service address on the authorization form, one per line, and upload bills for each.

**Does the authorization change my service?** No. It only allows AUR to request billing and rate information from your utilities.

**Can a school, city or county use AUR?** Yes. Public entities, schools, hospitals and churches are among our clients.

**Do I have to act on your findings?** No. You approve any action before anything changes.

**Someone referred me. Does that change anything?** No. Your review and terms are the same. Referral partners may earn a fee from AUR, never from you.
