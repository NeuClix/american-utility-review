# American Utility Review – Referral Partner Guide

This guide is for referral partners: people who introduce businesses and organizations to American Utility Review (AUR) and earn a reward when a referral becomes a client. It covers how the program works, how to use your dashboard and tools, the rules, and answers to common questions.

---

## 1. About American Utility Review

American Utility Review (AUR) is a team of independent commercial utility auditors. The business has been reviewing utility bills since 1986 (originally as Utility Review Consultants LLC).

**What AUR does:**

- Reviews electric, natural gas and water/sewer bills for billing errors and overcharges.
- Checks that the customer is on the proper rate schedule, based on the utility's tariffs and riders.
- Identifies refunds that may be owed and helps recover them.
- Recommends ways to save on utility costs going forward.

**Who AUR works with:** manufacturers, restaurants, hotels, hospitals, schools and universities, churches, local, county and state governments, agriculture, oil and gas producers, pipeline companies, retail and other businesses of all sizes, nationwide.

**How clients get started:** a business requests a free bill review on the website, uploads recent utility bills, and signs a Letter of Authorization so AUR can request billing history from its utility companies. AUR reviews the bills and shares what it finds. The client approves any action before anything changes. There is no upfront cost to request a review.

## 2. How the referral program works

1. **Sign up** at **aur.neuclix.com/refer.html**. It's free and takes about a minute.
2. **Get your personal link.** After signing up you go straight to your private dashboard and receive an email with your dashboard link. Your link looks like `https://aur.neuclix.com/?ref=ABC234`, where `ABC234` is your personal referral code.
3. **Share your link** with business owners and managers you know, by text, email, social media or in person using your QR code or flyer.
4. **They request a free review** through your link. The request is credited to you.
5. **AUR reviews their bills.** You can follow progress on your dashboard.
6. **You earn a reward** when your referral becomes an AUR client. The reward is released after the client's payment to AUR is received.

The current reward is shown at the top of your dashboard and on the Refer & Earn page.

---

## 3. Who can join

- Anyone 18 or older can join.
- **Employees and officials of public entities cannot receive rewards.** This includes schools, cities, counties, state agencies, and public hospitals and universities, and applies to referring any organization. It prevents conflicts of interest. Public employees are still welcome to request a free review for their own organization.
- You cannot refer yourself or an organization you own or control.
- AUR may decline or close any partner account.

## 4. Signing up

1. Go to **aur.neuclix.com/refer.html** and scroll to **Get your referral link**.
2. Enter your full name and email. Phone and company are optional.
3. Leave "I work for a school, city, county…" unchecked unless it applies to you. If it does, you can't join.
4. Tick the box to agree to the program terms, including telling people you may earn a referral fee.
5. Click **Get my link**. You land on your dashboard, and a welcome email with your private dashboard link is sent to you. If you don't see it, check your spam folder.

**Already signed up?** Enter the same email on the signup form and your dashboard link will be emailed to you again.

## 5. Your dashboard

Your dashboard is a private page at `aur.neuclix.com/partner.html?t=…`. The link in your welcome email opens it. Keep that link private: anyone who has it can see your dashboard.

### Stats
- **Link visits:** how many times your link has been opened.
- **Referrals:** how many review requests came through your link.
- **Became clients:** referrals that converted into AUR clients.
- **Earned / paid:** rewards earned so far, and how much has been paid.

### Your link
Your personal link, with a **Copy** button and one-tap sharing by email, text, LinkedIn, Facebook, X and WhatsApp. On a phone, the **Share…** button opens your phone's own share menu.

### QR code
A QR code of your link. Use **Download QR code** to save it for business cards, flyers or a counter sign. Anyone who scans it is credited to you.

### Marketing kit
- **Your personal flyer:** a one-page flyer with your QR code and link, ready to print or save as a PDF to email. It includes a line saying you are an AUR referral partner who may earn a referral fee.
- **Shared materials:** approved materials from AUR, such as audio explainers, infographics, slides and FAQ briefs, to download and share.

### Ready-made messages
Copy-and-send messages for a text, an email to a business owner, a LinkedIn or Facebook post, and a note for accountants and bookkeepers. Each includes the required note that you may earn a referral fee. Replace [Name] and [Your name] before sending.

### Your referrals
A list of every referral with its date, organization, status and reward.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **New** | The request was received. |
| **Qualified** | AUR is reviewing it. |
| **Converted** | It became an AUR client. Your reward is released once the client's payment to AUR is received. |
| **Paid** | Your reward has been paid. |
| **Not a fit** | It wasn't a fit for an audit this time. |

You get an email each time a referral's status changes, plus an email when someone requests a review through your link.

## 6. Install the app

You can add your dashboard to your phone or computer like an app. The app, called **AUR**, opens straight to your dashboard.

- **Android, and Chrome or Edge on a computer:** open your dashboard and tap **Install app** in the "Get the app" box, or use the browser's install option.
- **iPhone or iPad:** open your dashboard in **Safari**, tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**.

The app remembers you on each device where you've opened your dashboard link. To use it on another device, open the dashboard link from your welcome email on that device first.

## 7. How referral credit works

- When someone opens your link, their device remembers your code for **90 days**.
- If they request a review within those 90 days, on the same device and browser, the referral is credited to you, even if they come back to the website later without the link.
- If they open another partner's link afterward, the most recent link wins.
- If they switch devices, clear their browser data, or were already in contact with AUR, credit may not be recorded. AUR decides credit in good faith.
- Referrals from your own email address are flagged as possible self-referrals.

**Tip:** ask the person to use your link when they request the review, even if they've visited the website before.

## 8. Rules for promoting AUR

- **Always say you may earn a referral fee.** It's required by U.S. advertising rules (FTC), and it builds trust. The ready-made messages and your flyer already include it.
- **Describe AUR truthfully.** Don't promise or guarantee savings or refunds, and don't quote numbers AUR hasn't published.
- **Don't say you work for AUR** or speak on its behalf.
- **No spam.** Only contact people you know or who agreed to hear from you, and follow email and text-message laws.
- **Don't use AUR's name in paid search ads,** or create websites or social accounts that look like AUR's.
- **Don't name AUR's clients** unless AUR has published them.

---

## 9. Getting paid

- Rewards are earned when a referral becomes a client and **AUR has received the client's payment**.
- AUR pays rewards by a method it chooses, normally within 30 days of being paid.
- **AUR needs a completed IRS Form W-9 before your first payment**, and may issue a Form 1099 as the law requires. You are responsible for any taxes on your rewards.
- The reward amount in effect on the date of the referral applies.

## 10. Tips that work

- **Think about who pays large utility bills:** restaurant owners, plant and facility managers, farm and ranch operators, hotel managers, church administrators, property managers and office building owners.
- **Make it personal.** A direct text or email to someone you know works better than a public post.
- **Lead with "free" and "no upfront cost."** Requesting a review costs them nothing.
- **Accountants and bookkeepers** see their clients' utility bills every month and make great partners.
- **Use your flyer and QR code** at meetings, trade shows, chamber events and on counters.

## 11. Frequently asked questions

**Does it cost anything to join?** No.

**How much can I earn?** The current reward is shown on your dashboard and the Refer & Earn page. There is no limit on the number of referrals.

**When do I get paid?** After your referral becomes a client and AUR receives the client's payment, normally within 30 days of that.

**Why does my dashboard say "We couldn't open this dashboard"?** Your dashboard link may be incomplete, or your partner account may have been closed. Enter your email on the page to have your link sent again, or contact AUR.

**I lost my dashboard link.** Go to aur.neuclix.com/refer.html and sign up again with the same email. Your existing dashboard link will be emailed to you; a new account is not created.

**Someone said they used my link but it's not on my dashboard.** They may have used a different device or browser, cleared their browser data, or come to the website without your link more than 90 days after opening it. Contact AUR with the details.

**Can I refer my own business?** No, self-referrals don't earn rewards. You can still request a free review for your business.

**I work for a school district. Can I join?** You can't receive rewards, but your school can request a free review.

**Who sees my information?** AUR uses your details only to run the program and pay rewards. Your dashboard shows each referral's organization and status, but not their contact details or bills.

**How do I leave the program?** Email AUR and your account will be closed. Rewards for referrals that already became clients will still be paid.
