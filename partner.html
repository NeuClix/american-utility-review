Tips that work

  • Think about who pays big utility bills: restaurant owners, plant managers, farm and ranch operators, hotel managers, church administrators and office building owners.
  • Make it personal. A direct text or email to someone you know beats a public post.
  • Lead with “free” and “no upfront cost.” The review costs them nothing to find out.
  • Accountants and bookkeepers see their clients’ utility bills every month and make great partners.
  • Always say you may earn a referral fee. It’s required, and people trust you more for it.
